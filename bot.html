About the EAA-Scan bot

If you saw EAA-Scan/1.0 in your server logs: it loads a single public page in a headless browser, exactly as a visitor's browser would, and runs the open-source axe-core accessibility rules against it. It does not crawl your site, log in, fill forms or store page content. Only aggregate statistics are ever published, and sites are never named. It waits several seconds between requests and skips any site whose robots.txt disallows automated access.

Want to be excluded, or have a question? Please open an issue at github.com/Hanru269/eaa-scan/issues naming your domain and it will be excluded.

Automated scans are not legal advice and not a compliance certificate. Built with axe-core. Developer: Hanru Croucamp.
