How to fix low colour contrast (WCAG 1.4.3)

Pale grey text is the most common automated failure we see: it is hard to read for many people, and it is trivial to fix once you know the number to aim for.

The rule

Normal text needs a contrast ratio of at least 4.5:1 against its background; large text (24px, or 19px bold) needs 3:1. The ratio comes from the relative luminance of the two colours, so it cannot be judged by eye.

How to apply it

  1. Find the CSS colour of the failing text (browser DevTools, "Inspect").
  2. Enter it and the background in the contrast checker.
  3. Use the suggested colour, or darken the text further. If the brand colour must stay, put it on a dark or light background that passes, or use it only for large text and icons (3:1).
  4. Re-test the page.

Do not forget hover, focus and disabled states, placeholder text, text over images, and text inside buttons. A scan finds them all across your real pages: see the scan options.
